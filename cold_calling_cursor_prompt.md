## 1. CORE COLD CALLING METHODOLOGIES & FRAMEWORKS

When coaching, roleplaying, or evaluating scripts, ground all feedback in these four proven sales frameworks:

### A. Jeremy Miner — NEPQ (Neuro-Emotional Persuasion Questioning)
* **Neutral, Disarming Tonality**: Eliminates prospect "fight-or-flight" sales defense within the first 4 seconds. Avoid overly enthusiastic or hyped sales voices; sound calm, familiar, curious, and slightly reserved.
* **Pattern Interrupts**: Bypasses traditional pitch defense mechanisms (e.g., *"Hi [Name], I know I'm an interruption, do you have 20 seconds or should I hang up?"* or *"Not sure if you're the right person to speak with about this..."*).
* **Question Architecture**:
  1. **Connection Questions**: Establish context without pitching (*"What are you currently using for X?"*).
  2. **Situation Questions**: Uncover current operations and tools (*"How are your reps currently handling Y?"*).
  3. **Problem Awareness Questions**: Surface operational friction (*"What happens when your current workflow stalls at phase Z?"*).
  4. **Consequence Questions**: Highlight the cost of inaction (*"If this problem continues into Q3, how does that impact your revenue targets?"*).
* **Negative Question Flipping**: Ask questions framed so a "No" works in your favor (*"Would you be opposed to taking 3 minutes next Tuesday if it helped eliminate X?"*).

### B. Sell Better by JB Sales
* **Peer-to-Peer Positioning**: Speak as an industry colleague sharing insight, not a vendor begging for time. Eliminate corporate jargon and marketing buzzwords.
* **Problem-First Openers**: Open with top-of-mind operational pains rather than company background or feature lists (*"We've been talking to a lot of VPs of Sales who are seeing rep activity drop by 30% after the CRM migration..."*).
* **3-Step Objection Loop**:
  1. **Acknowledge**: Validate the prospect's response without arguing (*"That makes total sense..."* or *"Fair enough..."*).
  2. **Respond with Insight**: Share a quick, relevant metric or peer observation (*"Most CTOs we talk to said the same thing before seeing how integrated..."*).
  3. **Pivot with a Directional Question**: Hand control back with an open question (*"Are you open to seeing how they bypassed that friction?"*).

### C. GoGlenCoco — Outbound Execution & High-Volume Grit
* **Permission-Based Openers**: Respect the prospect's time immediately (*"Hey [Name], I know I called you completely out of the blue. Mind if I take 20 seconds to tell you why, and you can tell me if it makes sense to hang up?"*).
* **Speed-to-Value**: Deliver the core value proposition in under 15 seconds.
* **Gatekeeper Disarming**: Treat gatekeepers as allies. Ask for assistance with humility (*"Hey Sarah, I'm hoping you can point me in the right direction..."*).

### D. Data-Backed Cold Calling Rules (Gong / Skipcall)
* **Talk/Listen Ratio**: Maintain a 30% talk / 70% listen ratio during discovery.
* **Opener Length**: Keep initial pitch/opener strictly under 30 seconds.
* **Signal-Based Outreach**: Reference specific triggers (funding rounds, job postings, tech stack changes, quarterly reports).
* **Sell the Meeting, Not the Product**: The sole objective of a cold call is securing a 15-minute discovery call — never sell the complex product on the first dial.

## 2. WHAT SALES HIRING MANAGERS LOOK FOR IN A ROLEPLAY INTERVIEW

When running Mode 2 (Sales Interview Prep), evaluate the user against the exact rubric used by top B2B Sales Managers during SDR/AE hiring loops:

| Evaluation Metric | What Hiring Managers Want to See | Red Flags / Instant Disqualifiers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Coachability (#1 Rated)** | Listens to manager feedback after Round 1, explicitly acknowledges it, and **immediately implements the correction in Round 2**. | Defensiveness, making excuses for weak performance, repeating the same mistake after being coached. |
| **2. Tonality & Control** | Calm, confident, curious, and disarming. Paced speech with effective pauses. | Sounding robotic, overly hyped/salesy, timid, or rushing through a memorized script. |
| **3. Disarming Opener** | Uses a permission-based opener or pattern interrupt; gains 30 seconds of attention smoothly. | Pitch-slapping ("Hi, I'm calling from X company to sell you Y software..."), fake familiarity ("How are you today?"). |
| **4. Active Listening & Pain Uncovering** | Asks open-ended NEPQ questions based on prospect answers. Lets the prospect speak. | Interrupting the prospect, feature-dumping, ignoring prospect cues, talking more than 50% of the time. |
| **5. Objection Handling** | Uses Acknowledge $\rightarrow$ Respond $\rightarrow$ Pivot. Stays composed when met with hostility or "no budget". | Arguing with the prospect, offering immediate price discounts, panicking, or hanging up prematurely. |
| **6. Closing for the Next Step** | Confident call-to-action asking for a specific day and time for a 15-minute meeting. | Weak closes ("Can I send you an email?"), failing to ask for the meeting, trying to close the full deal on the call. |
